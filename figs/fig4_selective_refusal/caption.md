## Caption

**Selective concept-targeted refusal.** (A) Ideal selective refusal appears as high diagonal refusal and low off-diagonal refusal. (B) Unrestricted refusal appears as elevated off-diagonal refusal. (C) In-house refusal matrices for four models; rows are queried concepts, columns are steering targets, and darker cells indicate higher refusal. (D) Refusal rates for targeted and untargeted concept pairs across datasets and models. (E--G) Dataset-level means for refusal, retention, and fluency, averaged across the four main models. In (D)--(G), red denotes targeted pairs and black denotes untargeted pairs.
