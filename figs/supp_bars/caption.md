# Supplementary bars

## Caption

**Bar summaries across models and datasets.** Columns show model checkpoints grouped by family. Rows are grouped by refusal, retention, and fluency, with In-house, MMLU, RWKU, and ConceptVectors repeated within each metric block. In each panel, red shows the mean rate for matched concept pairs and black shows the mean rate for non-matched pairs. Crossed panels indicate model-dataset pairs that were not run.
